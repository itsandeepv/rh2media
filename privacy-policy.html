RH2 Media ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it and how we protect it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, company details, budget range and any message you send through our forms, email or WhatsApp.
  • Information collected automatically: basic technical data such as browser type, device, pages visited and approximate location, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the services you request.
  • To send proposals, invoices and service-related communication.
  • To improve our website and understand which content is useful.
  • To send occasional marketing updates, only where you have agreed. You can opt out at any time.

Legal basis and consent

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you submit a form or contact us, and for legitimate uses permitted by law. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us operate — such as form processing, email, hosting and analytics providers — under appropriate confidentiality obligations, or where required by law.

Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies and similar technologies for analytics and advertising measurement. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.

Data retention and security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law, and we use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect it.
