About us

We exist to make marketing accountable.

RH2 Media is a New Delhi-based digital marketing agency for Indian SMBs and startups who want growth they can measure, from a team they can trust.

Our story

Built for businesses that are tired of guesswork.

Before starting RH2 Media, our founders spent years running campaigns for brands of every size. One pattern kept repeating: small and mid-sized businesses were paying for marketing they could not measure, from agencies that disappeared once the contract was signed.

We set out to build the opposite — an agency where strategy is shared openly, every rupee is tracked and senior specialists stay close to the work. Our name reflects the idea we hold onto: turning attention into growth, and proving it every month.

Today we partner with clinics, real-estate developers, D2C brands, education companies, professional firms and B2B startups across India.
